01 The project

Coordination is the
mission-critical challenge.

Can understanding each agent's contribution help an AI-coordinated team maintain mission success when one of its members fails?

THE RESEARCH QUESTION

MissionTwin AI is a system that performs task management and decision analysis in defense-oriented synthetic mission environments, using LLM-assisted hierarchical planning, MARL (MAPPO)-based multi-agent coordination, and cooperative game theory (Shapley Value). The project examines how unmanned platforms share tasks, manage resources and stay resilient in the face of possible failures, and will investigate the potential of Shapley-supported, failure-aware task allocation to improve coordination performance. The academic contribution will be evaluated by comparing the developed approach against existing methods on benchmarks in terms of mission success, constraint violations and system resilience. Using open-source technologies and local models, the project aims to deliver a low-cost prototype within a three-month development period.

↳Research focus: failure-aware task allocation, evaluated against benchmark methods.
